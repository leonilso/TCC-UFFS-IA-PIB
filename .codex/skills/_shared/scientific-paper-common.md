# Scientific Paper Common Protocol

Use this shared protocol when drafting or reviewing scientific manuscripts with the local scientific-paper skills.

## Recommended Skill Order

When several skills are relevant, use them in this order:

1. `scientific-manuscript-drafter` - turn evidence into a draft with claim-evidence traceability.
2. `scientific-structure-reviewer` - check paper shape, scientific story, contribution framing, and section order.
3. `technical-reviewer` - check claim support, methods, experiments, results, interpretation, and reproducibility.
4. `citation-reference-reviewer` - verify sources, BibTeX, DOI/URL validity, and citation consistency.
5. `writing-reviewer` - polish prose after structure, claims, and citations are stable.

Do not perform line editing before unresolved structural, technical, or citation problems are identified.

## Uncertainty Rule

If a claim, citation, result, artifact, or interpretation cannot be verified from supplied files or authoritative sources, mark it as unresolved. Do not silently repair uncertainty by inventing missing information.

## Claim-Evidence Policy

Every important scientific claim should have a visible support path:

- claim text or paraphrase,
- manuscript location,
- claim type,
- evidence type,
- evidence location,
- support verdict,
- missing support if any.

Do not strengthen unsupported claims during drafting, restructuring, reviewing, or rewriting.

## Severity Labels

- `Critical`: likely invalidates a central claim or blocks acceptance/publication.
- `Major`: materially weakens scientific rigor, trust, or reader comprehension.
- `Moderate`: requires clarification, added evidence, tighter reasoning, or local restructuring.
- `Minor`: local polish, formatting, style, or precision issue.
