	Uncertainty challenges decision-making by public- and private-sector managers. This study compares the traditional econometric models VAR, ARIMA, DFM, and MIDAS with two artificial intelligence (AI)-based models: neural networks and Random Forest. It forecasts both the level and the growth rate of Brazil's real gross domestic product (GDP) at an annual frequency. The comparison considers execution time, qualitative interpretability, and predictive performance, assessed using root mean squared error (RMSE) and mean absolute error (MAE), to discuss how these models may inform economists' decision-making.
	
	\keywords{Artificial intelligence; Brazilian GDP; Quantitative methods; Forecasting models.}
